## Teaching

My goal is to give students a disciplined, structured process for reasoning independently about difficult political questions.

1. **Evaluate evidence**: students build evidence-based claims with data they collect themselves (*active learning*)
2. **Engage disagreement**: structured discussion with rotating partners (*think&ndash;pair&ndash;share*)
3. **Scaffolded assignments**: grades spread across many small assignments (*draft &rarr; feedback &rarr; revision*)
