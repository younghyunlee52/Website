During the 2025–2026 academic year, I served as a graduate mentor in the Undergraduate Research Apprenticeship Program (URAP) at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. I mentored Charlotte Rothrock, an undergraduate research apprentice who assisted with transcribing qualitative interview data from my fieldwork in Belgium and Germany. It was a rewarding experience to introduce an undergraduate student to the research process and further develop my mentoring skills.

More information about the program can be found [here](https://grad.illinois.edu/professional-development/graduate-college-mentoring-certificate/undergraduate-research).
