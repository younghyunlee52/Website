I was invited to participate in the two-day workshop Political Identities in Europe, hosted by the [Amsterdam Centre for European Studies (ACES)](https://aces.uva.nl/). I presented the research design phase of my project, *When Contact Matters: The Quality of Interpersonal Interaction and Immigrant Political Integration*. I am grateful to ACES for supporting my international travel to Amsterdam and providing accommodation throughout the workshop.

More information about the workshop can be found [here](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/aceseurope_workshop-politicalidentitiesineurope-activity-7263231581495543808-UsRP).
