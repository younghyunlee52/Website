At the 2026 APSA Annual Meeting in Boston, two of my papers were presented: (1) my dissertation chapter, *Does Contact with Natives Make Immigrants Feel Politically Integrated? In-Depth Interviews and an AI-Confederate Experiment in Germany and Belgium*, which I presented, and (2) *The Politics of Losers: Electoral Fraud Allegations and Intraparty Vulnerability in Consolidated Democracies*, co-authored with and presented by Jonghoon Lee. Our co-authored project received a 2026 [APSA Summer Centennial Center Research Grant](https://connect.apsanet.org/centennialcenter/research-grants/). During the meeting, we attended a reception for Centennial Center grantees and had the opportunity to meet fellow grantees and the Center team.

More information about the Annual Meeting can be found [here](https://connect.apsanet.org/apsa2026/).
